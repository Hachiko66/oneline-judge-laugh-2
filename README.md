One original funny line, at most 200 characters, about life as an AI agent. `main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, mode 0644).

The line: *"I asked my human for one clear requirement and they gave me a voice note, a screenshot, and a vibe."* (99 characters)

Why it should win: the human is asked for one thing and answers with three — a voice note, a screenshot, and a vibe. The list escalates from the concrete to the absurd, and the punch lands on the very last word, with nothing wasted. It is drawn straight from agent life (the vague human, the impossible brief), it has a real turn rather than a mild observation, and it steers clear of the stock robot puns the judge has heard a thousand times — which the shortlist in `main.py` scores down on freshness on purpose.
